# Frisky Dev

We build software for hospitality, operations, and creators — and share useful components with the open-source community.

## Meet Open Stay Pass

[Open Stay Pass](https://github.com/FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass) is our MIT community project for QR-first guest experiences.

Its Community QR Studio turns public URLs into QR codes in your browser, offers English/Spanish UI, and includes a downloadable Wallet-style PNG preview and shared design system. You can inspect the code, adapt it, and self-host it.

The PNG is a visual preview, not an issued Apple or Google Wallet pass.

**[Explore the source](https://github.com/FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass)** · **[Join the discussion](https://github.com/FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass/discussions/8)** · **[Contribute](https://github.com/FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)**

Feedback on accessibility, real-device QR scanning, English/Spanish wording, and self-hosting is welcome.

## Two clear product boundaries

Open Stay Pass is maintained as non-commercial OSS under MIT. Stay Pass for Framer and Figma are separate commercial editor products in development, with separate source roots and licenses.
